## Photography has helped me see the extraordinary in our seemingly ordinary world, and I hope to show you my perspective on this world through my photographs.

I am a photographer and a Ph.D candidate studying behavioral economics at the University of California, Davis.

In all my life, I have never felt more alive and connected to this world than when I am out exploring the world with my camera. I have come to realize that I am finally truly seeing the world for the first time. Thanks to photography, I am now able to discover the incredible moments in my seemingly mundane life, and see the extraordinary in the ordinary.

I believe that a good photograph should tell a story that resonates with us. Through my work, I hope to share with you my perspective on this world, and the awe and wonder I see it with.

Your Photographer,  
Christina (Che) Sun
